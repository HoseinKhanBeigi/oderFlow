How to read this

Side

Aggressive BUY Buyer crossed spread → hit resting sellers on the ask.

Aggressive SELL Seller crossed spread → hit resting buyers on the bid.

Size tiers (fixed $)

    Relative flags

    • LARGE — top ~5%
    • VERY_LARGE — top ~1%
    • EXTREME — top ~0.1%

    Events

    Bursts, large prints, market state changes, absorption, and liquidity pull / wall events appear in the events panel.

    Liquidity

    Each band shows how much % is left until that leverage is liquidated. After price reaches it, it says LIQUIDATED. Absorption means aggressive flow is being absorbed and price is stalling.

    Flow battle

    Positive delta is not a buyer win. Aggressive buyers vs passive sellers, and aggressive sellers vs passive buyers. Winner is who actually controls price.
